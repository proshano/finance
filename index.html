The model

The simulator forecasts monthly revenue, costs and staffing for a new clinical trial unit. Studies start at a constant rate, every active study recruits patients at a constant rate, and staffing rises with the number of active studies. The model calculates net profit for each month and accumulates it over the forecast.

All figures, charts and tables recalculate when an assumption changes. The six assumptions listed first have the largest effect on net profit at the default values. The remaining assumptions are in the three groups below them. Copy link stores the current assumptions in a URL that can be shared.

Summary measures

Break-even
The first month from which cumulative net profit remains at or above zero through the end of the forecast.
Net profit
Revenue net of institutional overhead, less all costs, summed over the forecast.
Funding requirement
The largest cumulative loss during the forecast, which the unit must fund from other sources.
Net profit, last 12 months
Net profit over the final 12 months of the forecast.

Assumptions

Calculations

Studies and patients

New studies per month = New studies per year ÷ 12

Active studies in month M = New studies per month × (M or the study duration, whichever is smaller)

Studies closed in month M = New studies per month, once M is greater than the study duration

Patients recruited = Active studies × Patients per study per month

Inflation and salary increases

Both apply annually. Year 1 covers months 1 to 12, year 2 covers months 13 to 24, and so on.

Inflation factor in year N = (1 + Annual inflation rate) ^ (N − 1)

Salary factor in year N = Inflation factor × (1 + Annual salary increase) ^ (N − 1)

With 3% inflation and a 2% salary increase:

Year 1: inflation factor 1.0000, salary factor 1.0000

Year 2: inflation factor 1.0300, salary factor 1.0506

Year 3: inflation factor 1.0609, salary factor 1.1038

Revenue

Revenue before overhead = (New studies × Revenue per study initiation + Patients recruited × Per patient payment + Studies closed × Revenue per study closeout) × Inflation factor

Institutional overhead = Revenue before overhead × Institutional overhead rate

Revenue = Revenue before overhead − Institutional overhead

Staff

Research assistants = Active studies ÷ Studies per research assistant

Coordinators = Active studies ÷ Studies per coordinator

Both are rounded up to whole positions unless fractional positions are allowed.

Salaries and benefits = Staff × Monthly salary × Salary factor × (1 + Benefits rate)

Severance accrual = Salaries and benefits × Severance accrual rate

Staff administration = Staff × (Administrative cost per staff member ÷ 12) × Inflation factor

Staff costs = Salaries and benefits + Severance accrual + Staff administration

Other costs

Study initiation costs = New studies × Cost per study initiation × Inflation factor

Per patient expenses = Patients recruited × Per patient expenses × Inflation factor

Study closeout costs = Studies closed × Cost per study closeout × Inflation factor

Fixed costs = Monthly fixed costs × Inflation factor

Net profit

Net profit = Revenue − Staff costs − Other costs

Cumulative net profit in month M = Sum of net profit for months 1 to M

Simplifying assumptions

  • New studies start at a constant rate, so the model counts fractions of a study. Four studies per year is one third of a study each month.
  • Every active study recruits at the same rate for its full duration.
  • The unit earns the per patient payment and incurs the per patient expenses in the month it recruits the patient.
  • The institution deducts its overhead from all revenue before the unit receives it.
  • Inflation and salary increases take effect annually, from the first month of year 2.
  • Staff numbers do not fall during the forecast.
  • The model records severance accrual as a staff cost in the month of accrual.
